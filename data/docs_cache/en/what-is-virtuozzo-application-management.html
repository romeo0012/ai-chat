What is Virtuozzo Application Management? Virtuozzo Application Management is a Platform as a Service (PaaS) designed to help developers and businesses build, deploy, and manage applications in the cloud with ease. Its main purpose is to automate and simplify the entire application lifecycle - from development to deployment, scaling, and ongoing management - so teams can focus on innovation instead of infrastructure. Who is it for? Virtuozzo Application Management is ideal for developers, DevOps teams, and businesses looking for a flexible, scalable, and cost-effective cloud platform. Its container-based approach is perfect for modern microservices architectures. It also supports cloud-native and traditional applications, making it suitable for everyone, from small startups to large enterprises. What does it offer? One-click Application Deployment: Launch popular applications from a built-in marketplace in seconds. Includes various solutions like WordPress and Kubernetes clusters. Multi-language Support: Supports a wide range of regularly updated software stacks, including application servers (for Java, PHP, Node.js, Python, Ruby, Go, .NET), databases (MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, etc.), and more. Containerization: Each stack runs in its own isolated container, ensuring security and resource efficiency. Automated Scaling: Automatically adjusts resources up or down based on real-time demand, ensuring performance and cost-efficiency.
